## Key Concepts

- **Cap table:** Ownership ledger of shares and percentages.
- **Fully diluted basis:** Assumes all options/convertibles are exercised.
- **Option pool shuffle:** Pre-money pool creation that dilutes founders.
- **Pro forma cap table:** Post-transaction ownership model.
- **Conversion mechanics:** Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE) notes converting into equity.

## Main Content

### Chapter 1: Definitions and dilution mechanics

Cap tables track **ownership**, **options**, and **dilution** across rounds.

- **Fully diluted shares:** All shares including options and convertibles.
- **Pro rata rights:** Right to maintain ownership in future rounds.
- **Option pool shuffle:** Pool size and timing shift dilution.

<Callout type="note" title="Dilution is not always bad">
If new capital increases enterprise value faster than ownership falls, founders can still win.
</Callout>

### Chapter 4: Worked example with numbers

**Scenario:** Seed and Series A with a 15% option pool.

Use the round-by-round table to translate valuation and new money into dilution checkpoints for founders and new investors.

| Round | Pre-money | New Money | Post-money | Founder % (fully diluted) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | \$6M | \$2M | \$8M | 65% |
| Series A | \$18M | \$6M | \$24M | 45% |

**Takeaway:** Each round compresses founder ownership; the size of the new check and the pre-money valuation jointly determine the drop.

**Cap table after Series A (simplified)**

The cap table below converts the round math into a clean ownership snapshot you can sanity-check with stakeholders.

| Holder | Ownership |
| --- | --- |
| Founders | 45% |
| Seed investors | 20% |
| Series A investors | 25% |
| Option pool (unallocated) | 10% |

**Takeaway:** A simplified cap table is a communication tool—use it to align on who owns what before moving on to preferences and control rights.

## Worked Examples

### In-class activity: “Dilution Dash”

- **GameCo simulation:** founders start with 100k shares (100%).
- **Round 1:** Angel invests \$500k for 20% → founders drop to 80%.
- **Round 2:** Create 10% option pool → founders to 72%, angel to 18%, pool 10%.
- **Round 3:** Series A investor takes 25% post → founders ~54%, angel ~13.5%, pool ~7.5%.
- **Round 4:** Optional down round shows anti-dilution effects.
- **Debrief:** Percentages drop but company value grows; plan pool size and valuation.

### Case study: UrbanGardens dilution journey

- **Seed:** \$500k on \$4.5M pre (\$5M post), 5% pre-money pool; founders drop from 90% to ~76.5%.
- **Series A:** \$3M on \$15M pre; pool topped to 10% post; Series A VC ~20%.
- **Series B dilemma:** \$10M on \$40M pre with another pool refresh; founders risk falling below 40%.
- **Trade-offs:** Raise less to reduce dilution vs. raise more to hit milestones faster.

## Pitfalls

<Callout type="warning" title="Pitfalls to Avoid">
- Ignoring fully diluted ownership when discussing percentages.
- Underestimating option pool dilution or SAFE cap effects.
- Failing to model future rounds and control thresholds.
- Over-focusing on percentage instead of absolute value creation.
</Callout>

## Practice Questions

**Question:** What is included in fully diluted shares?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** All common, preferred, options, and convertibles.
</details>

**Question:** Why do investors seek pro rata rights?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** To maintain ownership in high-performing companies.
</details>

**Question:** If a 10% option pool is added pre-money, who bears the dilution?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** Founders (and existing shareholders) bear it first.
</details>

**Question:** If pre-money is \$8M and you raise \$2M, what is post-money?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** \$10M.
</details>

**Question:** Why can SAFEs complicate cap table forecasts?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** Conversion happens later and can be at variable caps/discounts.
</details>

**Question:** When is dilution acceptable?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** When capital increases enterprise value faster than ownership decreases.
</details>
