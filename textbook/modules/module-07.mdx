## Key Concepts

- **Cap table:** Ownership ledger of shares and percentages.
- **Fully diluted basis:** Assumes all options/convertibles are exercised.
- **Option pool shuffle:** Pre-money pool creation that dilutes founders.
- **Pro forma cap table:** Post-transaction ownership model.
- **Conversion mechanics:** Simple Agreement for Future Equity (SAFE) notes converting into equity.

## Main Content

### Chapter 1: Definitions and dilution mechanics

Cap tables track **ownership**, **options**, and **dilution** across rounds.

- **Fully diluted shares:** All shares including options and convertibles.
- **Pro rata rights:** Right to maintain ownership in future rounds.
- **Option pool shuffle:** Pool size and timing shift dilution.

<Callout type="note" title="Dilution is not always bad">
If new capital increases enterprise value faster than ownership falls, founders can still win.
</Callout>

### Pool timing explainer (pre- vs. post-money pool)

**Setup:** Founders have **8,000,000 shares**, raise **\$2M on an \$8M pre** (post \$10M). Target a **10% option pool**.

| Pool timing | Founder % | Investor % | Pool % | What changes? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pre-money pool** (created before financing) | 70.0% | 20.0% | 10.0% | Founders absorb pool dilution upfront. |
| **Post-money pool** (created after financing) | 72.0% | 18.0% | 10.0% | Founders + investors share the pool dilution. |

**Numeric detail (pre-money pool):** Total post shares = 11,428,571. Founders 8,000,000 (70.0%), investors 2,285,714 (20.0%), pool 1,142,857 (10.0%).

**Numeric detail (post-money pool):** Investor buys 2,000,000 shares at \$1.00. After adding 1,111,111 pool shares, total = 11,111,111. Founders 72.0%, investors 18.0%, pool 10.0%.

**Takeaway:** Pool timing is a negotiation lever—pre-money pools protect new investors, post-money pools spread dilution.

### Pro rata decision mini-case

**Case: BrightLeaf Robotics**
- **Today:** Founders 60%, Seed fund 20%, Angel syndicate 10%, Option pool 10%.
- **Series A:** \$8M on \$24M pre (post \$32M). New investors want **25% post**.
- **Pro rata:** Seed fund has the right to maintain its 20% ownership.

**Decision fork**
- **Allow pro rata:** Seed fund invests \$6.4M (20% of the \$32M post) and keeps 20%. Founders drop to **45%**, angels to **7.5%**, pool to **7.5%**.
- **Limit pro rata:** Seed fund invests \$3.2M (half its right). Seed drops to **10%**, founders land at **50%**, angels at **10%**, pool at **10%**, new investors increase to **30%**.

**Founder takeaway:** Allowing pro rata can preserve supportive insiders but increases new-money size or founder dilution; limiting it preserves founder % but can strain investor relationships.

### Dilution risk management checklist

- [ ] Model every round on a **fully diluted** basis (include SAFEs and options).
- [ ] Tie **option pool size** to a hiring plan, not a default %.
- [ ] Negotiate **pool timing** (pre vs. post) and quantify the impact.
- [ ] Track **ownership thresholds** (50%, 33%, 10%) for control and veto.
- [ ] Pressure-test **downside cases** (down round, flat round, bridge).
- [ ] Decide **pro rata policy** early and communicate it in the data room.
- [ ] Revisit dilution quarterly as valuations and hiring change.

## Worked Examples

### In-class activity: “Dilution Dash”

- **GameCo simulation:** founders start with 100k shares (100%).
- **Round 1:** Angel invests \$500k for 20% → founders drop to 80%.
- **Round 2:** Create 10% option pool → founders to 72%, angel to 18%, pool 10%.
- **Round 3:** Series A investor takes 25% post → founders ~54%, angel ~13.5%, pool ~7.5%.
- **Round 4:** Optional down round shows anti-dilution effects.
- **Debrief:** Percentages drop but company value grows; plan pool size and valuation.

### Case study: UrbanGardens dilution journey

- **Seed:** \$500k on \$4.5M pre (\$5M post), 5% pre-money pool; founders drop from 90% to ~76.5%.
- **Series A:** \$3M on \$15M pre; pool topped to 10% post; Series A VC ~20%.
- **Series B dilemma:** \$10M on \$40M pre with another pool refresh; founders risk falling below 40%.
- **Trade-offs:** Raise less to reduce dilution vs. raise more to hit milestones faster.

## Pitfalls

<Callout type="warning" title="Pitfalls to Avoid">
- Ignoring fully diluted ownership when discussing percentages.
- Underestimating option pool dilution or SAFE cap effects.
- Failing to model future rounds and control thresholds.
- Over-focusing on percentage instead of absolute value creation.
</Callout>

## Practice Questions

**Question:** What is included in fully diluted shares?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** All common, preferred, options, and convertibles.
</details>

**Question:** Why do investors seek pro rata rights?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** To maintain ownership in high-performing companies.
</details>

**Question:** If a 10% option pool is added pre-money, who bears the dilution?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** Founders (and existing shareholders) bear it first.
</details>

**Question:** If pre-money is \$8M and you raise \$2M, what is post-money?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** \$10M.
</details>

**Question:** Why can SAFEs complicate cap table forecasts?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** Conversion happens later and can be at variable caps/discounts.
</details>

**Question:** When is dilution acceptable?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** When capital increases enterprise value faster than ownership decreases.
</details>

**Question:** If founders own 8,000,000 of 12,307,692 shares, what is their ownership percentage?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** 65.0%.
</details>

**Question:** Why might founders prefer a post-money option pool?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** It spreads pool dilution across founders and new investors instead of founders alone.
</details>

**Question:** In a \$24M post-money round, what % ownership does a \$6M investor receive?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** 25%.
</details>

**Question:** What is the main trade-off of granting full pro rata rights to early investors?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** It preserves insider ownership but can reduce founder % or increase total new money required.
</details>

**Question:** Why do cap tables track fully diluted shares rather than just issued shares?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** Because options, SAFEs, and convertibles can become shares and materially change ownership.
</details>

**Question:** When would a founder accept more dilution?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** When the added capital significantly raises enterprise value or accelerates milestones.
</details>

**Question:** What does a pool “top-up” do to existing shareholders?  
<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** It increases total shares, diluting existing holders to restore the pool to a target %.
</details>
