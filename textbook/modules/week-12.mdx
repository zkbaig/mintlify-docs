## Learning objectives

- Identify macro trends reshaping venture capital (AI, climate, alternative finance).
- Explain how fund structures and investor types are evolving.
- Evaluate democratization trends such as crowdfunding and tokenization.
- Anticipate how exit timelines and liquidity mechanisms may change.

## Key concepts + definitions

- **Revenue-based financing (RBF):** Capital repaid as a % of revenue.
- **Venture debt:** Non-dilutive debt used to extend runway.
- **Rolling funds:** Continuous fundraising model for GPs.
- **Continuation funds:** Vehicles that extend holding periods for winners.
- **Tokenization:** Blockchain-based representations of equity or revenue shares.

## Main content

### Topic narrative

- VC is evolving with sector shifts (AI and climate tech) and new financing models.
- **Climate tech:** Resilient funding supported by policy (e.g., IRA) and blended finance.
- **AI:** Massive share of global VC dollars; AI also changes VC workflows via data tools.
- **VC tech:** Algorithmic scouting, due diligence automation, and data-driven decisioning.
- **Alternative financing:** RBF, venture debt, crowdfunding, and tokenization broaden options.
- **Crowdfunding:** Reg CF growth (~$340M in 2024) signals democratization.
- **Tokenization/Web3:** Potential for secondary liquidity under tighter regulation.
- **Fund structures:** Rise of solo GPs, rolling funds, micro-funds, and specialized vehicles.
- **Corporate VC:** Nearly half of deal value in 2024 involved corporates.
- **Diversity and inclusion:** Stronger focus from LPs and dedicated funds.
- **Exit trends:** Longer private timelines, more secondaries, potential IPO rebound in 2025.

## Pitfalls / common mistakes

- Treating new financing models as replacements rather than complements.
- Ignoring regulatory constraints for tokens and crowdfunding.
- Overconcentrating in hype sectors without diligence on unit economics.

## Quick checks (interactive)

- **Which sectors will dominate VC?** AI and climate tech are leading growth areas.
- **How will fund structures evolve?** More rolling funds, solo GPs, and multi-vehicle firms.
- **What role will democratization play?** Crowdfunding and tokenization expand access.
- **How will exits change?** Longer timelines with more secondaries and potential IPO rebound.
- **What external factors disrupt VC?** Rates, geopolitics, and regulation.

## Summary + key formulas

- VC is diversifying across sectors, structures, and funding instruments.
- AI and climate tech are long-term pillars requiring new expertise and capital stacks.
- Liquidity innovation (secondaries, continuation funds) is becoming standard.

## Practice questions

### Discussion questions and teaching notes

- **How should a fund balance AI and climate investments?**
  - Teaching note: Compare capital intensity, timelines, and policy tailwinds.
- **What alternative financing is best for steady-revenue startups?**
  - Teaching note: RBF or venture debt can reduce dilution.
- **Will tokenization become mainstream?**
  - Teaching note: Adoption depends on regulation and investor protections.
- **How will LP expectations shape fund models?**
  - Teaching note: LPs will reward transparency, DPI, and specialization.
