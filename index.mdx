<Steps>
  <Steps.Step title="Read the concept primer">
    Skim the opening section of each week to learn the frameworks and vocabulary.
  </Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Apply the decision checklist">
    Use the week’s questions to stress-test your financing strategy and metrics.
